Finding 01

Holdout signal is real

XGBoost achieved 2.30°F, 3.76°F, and 4.38°F MAE; the LSTM achieved 3.14°F, 4.06°F, and 4.48°F across H=1, H=3, and H=5.

Finding 02

Publication policy dominated error

The unanchored live model substantially outperformed the historically anchored canonical forecast in the committed attribution tape.

Finding 03

Intervals require qualified language

Conformalized test coverage improved sharply, but nonstationarity and validation reuse support the label calibrated risk band rather than an unconditional 90% guarantee.
